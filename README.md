# Hourbase

**Track your time. Know your worth.**

Hourbase is a time-tracking and billable-hours product for freelancers and independent professionals. It gives every hour a home — across clients, projects, rates, and earnings — so working time is no longer reconstructed from notes, spreadsheets, or memory.

Live marketing site: [https://hourbase-website.vercel.app](https://hourbase-website.vercel.app)

Product app: [https://hourbase.vercel.app](https://hourbase.vercel.app)

## Product

Hourbase is built for people who bill by the hour: designers, developers, consultants, writers, and other independent operators.

It helps them:

- Track time with a live timer or add hours manually
- Organize work by client and project, with rates, budgets, and activity in one place
- Separate billable from non-billable time
- See what tracked hours are worth, using global, client, or project rates
- Review simple reports instead of reconstructing the week at month-end

The product is a focused workspace, not a generic project-management suite. The goal is financial and time clarity: what you worked, for whom, and what that time is worth.
